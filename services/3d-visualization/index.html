WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY

Our 3D Rendering Process

PHASE 01

Geometry Setup & Camera Angles

Building or importing 3D models, framing key camera angles, and establishing spatial composition for client approval.

PHASE 02

Materials & Lighting Setup

Applying high-resolution PBR textures, configuring sunlight/HDRI environments, and fine-tuning interior lighting fixtures.

PHASE 03

High-Res Rendering & Color Grading

Executing high-pass render passes, compositing entourage elements, and completing professional color grading.
