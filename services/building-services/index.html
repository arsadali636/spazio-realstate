ENGINEERING INTEGRATION

Clash-Free Mechanical Coordination

By auditing hard and soft clashes between mechanical ducts, electrical trays, plumbing risers, and structural beams, we prevent expensive site re-work during installation.

  • MEP Platforms Revit MEP, Navisworks Manage, AutoCAD MEP
  • Disciplines Coordinated HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Firefighting, Drainage
  • Output Formats RVT Central Models, DWG Shop Drawings, NWD Audit Sets
  • Site Value Zero Spatial Conflicts & Precise Sleeve Location Drawings
BUILDER WORK & SLEEVE DETAILS

Pre-Pour Penetration Drawings

Accurate wall and slab penetration sleeve drawings to ensure structural pour teams embed duct and pipe penetrations in exact locations prior to concrete casting.

WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY

Our MEP Coordination Workflow

PHASE 01

Space Reservation & Schematic Review

Evaluating schematic single-line diagrams, headroom limits, ceiling voids, and defining main MEP riser shafts.

PHASE 02

3D Ductwork & Conduit Modeling

Developing 3D Revit MEP models for HVAC ducts, cable trays, pipe racks, and equipment clearance spaces.

PHASE 03

Navisworks Clash Audit & Release

Running multi-disciplinary clash matrices, clearing spatial overlaps, and issuing coordinated installation drawings.
