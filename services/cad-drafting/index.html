Input Analysis & Setup

Reviewing client sketches, markups, or PDFs, configuring custom CTB pen tables, scale settings, and layer structures.

PHASE 02

2D Drafting & Detailing

Executing precise 2D geometry, dimensioning, annotations, wall type tags, doors/windows schedules, and sectional callouts.
