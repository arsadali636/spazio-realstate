TECHNICAL MATRIX

BIM Execution & Technical Standards

Our BIM team works as an extension of your office, connecting directly to your BIM 360 / Autodesk Construction Cloud environments under strict Quality Assurance protocols.

  • Primary Software Platform Autodesk Revit, Navisworks Manage, AutoCAD
  • Level of Detail (LOD) LOD 100, 200, 300, 350, 400, 500
  • BIM Protocols & Standards ISO 19650, PAS 1192, US National BIM Standard
  • Core Deliverables RVT Central Models, NWD Clash Reports, COBie Data
POINT CLOUD TO BIM EXPERTISE

Scan to BIM & Renovation Modeling

We convert raw 3D laser scan data (E57, PTS, RCP format) into millimeter-accurate Revit BIM models. Perfect for heritage restoration, adaptive reuse, and complex interior renovation projects.

WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY

Our BIM Delivery Workflow

PHASE 01

BIM Setup & BEP Alignment

Establishing shared coordinate systems, project templates, Revit family libraries, and BIM Execution Plan (BEP) parameters.

PHASE 02

Multi-Disciplinary LOD Modeling

Developing architectural, structural, and MEP Revit models to specified LOD levels with parametric detail and data parameters.

PHASE 03

Clash Matrix & Coordination Audit

Running automated clash detection in Navisworks, resolving spatial conflicts, and issuing coordinated 2D drawing sheets.
