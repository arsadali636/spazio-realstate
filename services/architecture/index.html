WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY

Our Architectural Design Process

PHASE 01

Concept & Spatial Planning

Feasibility evaluation, initial spatial zoning, site response planning, and preliminary architectural massing concept generation.

PHASE 02

Design Development

Refining spatial plans, integrating facade design elements, finalizing materials, and coordinating structural grid alignment.

PHASE 03

Working Drawings & Documentation

Extracting comprehensive construction drawing packages including fully dimensioned floor plans, elevations, sections, and callouts.
